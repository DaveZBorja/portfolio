Services

I provide a wide range of services focused on electronics, embedded systems, and innovation-driven solutions. Whether you're working on a personal project, a school requirement, or an industrial application, I offer hands-on expertise to help bring your ideas to life with precision and reliability.

Custom Circuit Design & Prototyping

Design and develop electronic circuits tailored to your specific project needs. From conceptual schematics to functional prototypes, I create reliable and efficient circuits for applications such as automation, sensing, power control, and more. This service includes component selection, simulation (if needed), and breadboard or PCB-based prototyping for real-world testing and refinement.

Automation Projects (Home & Agriculture)

Design and implement automation systems that improve convenience, productivity, and efficiency. Whether you're automating household functions like lighting, appliances, and security, or developing smart solutions for agricultural tasks such as irrigation, environmental monitoring, or livestock management — I provide fully customized, Arduino or ESP-based automation tailored to your goals. Includes sensor integration, actuator control, wireless access, and programmable logic to fit your specific requirements.

Microcontroller Programming (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi Pico)

Develop and program intelligent systems using popular microcontrollers like Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi Pico. I offer custom firmware development for a wide range of applications including automation, sensor integration, wireless communication, and real-time control. Whether it's writing efficient code in C/C++, MicroPython, or Arduino IDE, I ensure reliable performance and flexible functionality tailored to your project's needs.

Networking Setup & Configuration

Design, configure, and optimize reliable networks for home, office, or industrial environments. I specialize in setting up routers, switches, VLANs, and wireless access points using devices like MikroTik, Cisco, and TP-Link. Services include IP addressing, DHCP, NAT, port forwarding, and bandwidth control. I also implement VLANs for secure network segmentation, optimize Wi-Fi coverage, and troubleshoot slow or unstable connections. Whether you need a simple setup or a segmented network for IoT, management, and clients — I deliver stable and secure solutions tailored to your needs.

Project Troubleshooting & Reverse Engineering

Identify and resolve issues in existing electronic circuits, devices, or projects that aren't functioning as expected. I offer in-depth troubleshooting for hardware and firmware problems, including signal tracing, component testing, and logic verification. For undocumented or legacy systems, I provide reverse engineering services — analyzing circuit boards or systems to recreate schematics, understand functionality, or make necessary modifications and upgrades. Ideal for repair, replication, or academic research.

AutoCAD Drafting & 3D SketchUp Modeling

Create precise and professional technical drawings and 3D models for electronics, enclosures, and mechanical layouts. I offer 2D AutoCAD drafting for schematics, wiring diagrams, and equipment layouts, ideal for documentation and fabrication. Using SketchUp, I design custom 3D models of project enclosures, device housings, and concept visualizations to help bring your ideas to life in a realistic and functional format. Perfect for prototyping, presentations, and physical implementation.
